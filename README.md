## Before you trust any rebate platform with your account

A short list, because the failure modes here are predictable:

- **Confirm the rate inside your own dashboard, not in an ad.** The only number that matters is the one attached to your account after binding.
- **No platform should ask you to deposit funds to "unlock" a rebate.** A rebate platform earns from exchange commissions. If money has to leave your wallet to activate a rate, you're looking at a different kind of business.
